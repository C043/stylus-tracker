Catalog images go in `<brand-slug>/<model-slug>.webp` (see `src/lib/catalog/turntables.ts`).
Only add images we may redistribute and list each one here with its source and license.

find; the **Shows** column says which. Replace any of them with a better licensed photo (your
own counts) and update its row. Images are resized to 600px and converted to WebP; CC BY-SA
